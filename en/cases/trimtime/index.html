CASE STUDY · TRIMTIME · DOG GROOMING SALON

The schedule out of her head, into one app.

TrimTime is a dog grooming salon with one owner and a full schedule. That schedule lived in her head: who comes back every how many weeks, which dog takes how long, who can only come on Wednesdays. We took that system out and put it into one app, together with a real website and a client area.

100+dogs with their own grooming rhythm in it
1 switchto open online booking
01

Where it started

Not every project starts with an audit day. Here it started with three things she put on the table herself: the schedule, the website and the socials.

The schedule lived in her head. She knew who had to come back every how many weeks and who could come on which day, and with every new appointment the puzzle started again. There was no real website. There was a free video people signed up for, but whoever requested it never heard anything after that. And social media felt like an obligation, while every grooming session naturally produces a before and after.

What I saw was this: everything she needed already existed. Clients, an offer, followers, a good reputation. Only the system around it was missing.

02

The proposal

For a business this size, a full audit day is too much. I made her a tailored proposal in three steps: a booking app with a client system, an email sequence behind her free video, and socials that mostly run by themselves.

She chose the foundation, and made it bigger: one app that brings her website, her bookings and her clients together. The rest can come later, when she wants it.

03

The hardest part: her rules

Buying a booking system is easy. But no standard system knows the rules of a grooming salon. A small dog doesn't take as long as a big one. You can't just schedule five large dogs in a row. And not every client can come at any time.

Those rules were not written down anywhere. So we wrote them down. One document with questions that she filled in herself, at her own pace. The booking logic is built on that.

04

What we built.

One app, with a public side and a side for her and her clients:

01The website

Her services, the workshops, the free video and a waiting list for new clients. In her own style, with her own photos. It is live.

02The client area

Every client has an account with their dog in it: the grooming rhythm, the days that work, and messages with photos.

03Booking by her rules

The system knows how long each dog takes and how many fit in a day. Clients only see the slots that really work.

04Her own admin

The schedule, notes per appointment, who didn't show up, and all the settings. She changes hours, prices and texts herself.

05Emails that send themselves

A confirmation, a reminder and a follow-up, without her having to think about it.

06One switch for launch

Online booking is ready and opens with one switch in her settings. Until then clients see a neat page saying it is coming.

05

How we worked together

A short check-in every week and a WhatsApp group for quick questions. Two style proposals to choose from, and then feedback that was very concrete: a softer colour, different photos, a less classic typeface, the free video more front and centre.

That is how it should be. It is her business, it has to feel like her business.

06

Where it stands today

I am not going to pretend there are results that aren't there yet. This is where things are:

The website is live.For the first time a place of her own that explains what she does and what it costs.
The waiting list works.New clients sign up instead of sending message after message.
The knowledge is in a system.The rules only she knew, the app now knows too.
Her clients are in it.With their dog and their grooming rhythm, ready to book themselves.
She picks the moment.Online booking opens in January, when it suits her.
The app is hers.If we ever stop working together, everything moves to her own accounts.
