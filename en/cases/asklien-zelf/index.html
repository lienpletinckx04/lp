CASE STUDY · ASKLIEN.AI · MY OWN BUSINESS

I don't build anything I don't use myself.

This is an odd case study, because the client is me. It is also the most honest one. If I ask you to trust AI inside your business, you should get to see how it runs in mine. AskLien is a small team, with me as the founder. What follows is how a small team handles the work of a much bigger one.

01

Why I am showing this

Every consultant says AI saves time. Almost none of them show their own kitchen. I think you should be able to check whether someone does what she sells.

There is another reason. Everything I build for a client, I built for myself first. The hub where clients follow their project, the status reports, the chatbot monitoring. My own business is where I find out what works before I put it anywhere else.

02

One project, from inquiry to invoice

Take an AI audit. This is what happens, and where an agent does the work:

Preparation. A skill gets everything ready for the audit day: what I already know about the business, the questions I need to ask, the tools they use.

The report. After the audit day a second skill turns my notes and the recording into a report in my usual structure. I rewrite, sharpen and decide what stays in.

The agreement. A generator drafts the proposal and the agreement, in my style and with my terms.

The project. The client gets a project in my hub, with phases and tasks. Every meeting comes in automatically, notes included.

The follow-up. Every week a status report is ready with what got done and what is open, per person.

The invoice. There is a skill behind that too.

03

What I deliberately do myself

The audit day itself. No agent sits in between. Watching how people work, asking follow-up questions, sensing where things rub: that is the job.

And reading before sending. A skill writes a first version, I decide what goes out. The weekly email to my members is prepared as a draft every Monday morning. It only leaves once I have read it.

That is my rule for every agent I build, for myself and for clients: the agent does the prep work, a person makes the call.

05

What I learned from this

Starting small beats dreaming big. My first skill did one thing. Only once I was using it every week did the second one come.

And not everything has to be an agent. The payment that creates an account is a plain workflow. No AI needs to think about that. The skill is knowing when you need which. That is exactly what I come to figure out in an audit at your business.
