How an AI audit works

Most projects start the same way, and it doesn't take months to get going:

01Intro call

You tell me what you do and where you're stuck. I'll tell you straight whether an audit makes sense or whether something smaller would do the job.

02One day on site

I come to where the work happens and watch. How does work come in? Who decides what? Where does the time go? Which tools are you already paying for?

03The report

Within a week you get a report: where time and money are leaking, your priorities in order, a review of your tools, and concrete proposals in more than one size.

04You decide

Do all of it, part of it, or none of it. If you move ahead with a build, the audit fee is credited in full.

05We build in phases

Your team tests as we go. Nothing ships until the people who'll use it have tried it.

06Retainer

If you want it to keep running and keep growing, we look after it on a fixed monthly retainer.
