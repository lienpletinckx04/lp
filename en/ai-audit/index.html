Questions I get a lot

What does an AI audit cost?

An AI audit costs $2,000. That covers one live audit day inside your business and a report with concrete proposals in two versions. If you move on to a build afterwards, the audit fee is fully credited.

What is the difference between the audit and the AI Setup?

The audit is for companies that first want to know where AI actually pays off: I spend a day with your team and you get a report with proposals. The AI Setup ($1,500, fully remote) is for people who want a working setup right away: I review your processes, build three custom skills and hand everything over in a walkthrough session.

Do you only work with Claude?

No. I use the best tool for the job. Often that is Claude, but ChatGPT, automations and the tools you already use are all on the table.

Does my team need AI experience?

No. I start from how you work today. Everything is explained in plain language, and at handover I make sure your team can run it without me.

Can we work together if we are not in Belgium?

Yes. The AI Setup and most builds run fully remote, in English or Dutch. For an audit day I come on site in Belgium, and in Miami by arrangement. Anywhere else we do the audit over video with screen sharing.

What happens after the build?

If you want everything to keep up with new tools and features, I keep your setup running on a fixed retainer. Small changes are included, new features are scoped separately so you always know what you pay for.
