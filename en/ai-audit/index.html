How I work

I'm less interested in what AI could do than in getting it running. I've worked with bakeries, accountants, marketing agencies and solo founders, and the approach is always the same. I look at how you work today and find the spots where AI will save real time or money, which usually aren't the ones that sound impressive in a meeting.

I use whatever tool fits the job. That's often Claude, and sometimes it's something else. I explain everything in plain English so your team keeps using it after I'm gone.

Questions I get a lot

What does an AI audit cost?

An AI audit costs $2,000. That covers one day on site at your business and a report with concrete proposals in two versions. If you move ahead with a build afterward, the audit fee is credited in full.

What's the difference between the audit and the AI Setup?

The audit is for companies that want to know where AI will pay off before they commit to anything. I spend a day with your team, and you get a report with proposals. The AI Setup ($1,500, fully remote) is for people who want a working setup right away. I review how you work, build three custom skills and walk you through everything at the end.

Do you only work with Claude?

No. I use whatever fits the job. That's often Claude, but ChatGPT, automations and the tools you already have are all options.

Does my team need AI experience?

No. I start with how you work today and explain everything in plain English. Before we wrap up, I make sure your team can run it without me.

Do you work with businesses across the US?

Yes. The AI Setup and most builds are fully remote. For an audit day I come to you in Miami or in Belgium. Anywhere else in the US, we do the audit over video with screen sharing.

What happens after the build?

If you want your setup to keep up with new tools and features, we keep it running on a fixed monthly retainer. Small changes are included. New features get scoped and priced separately, so you always know what you're paying for.
